## 4.1 Task와 의도된 풀이

**질문**
"In the NCATS PubChem compound database for Food Additive Status classification, find the compound that has a molecular weight of 100 g/mol or less, 6 heavy atoms, 1 or fewer hydrogen bond acceptors, and a complexity between 10 and 15. Of the shared gene-chemical co-occurrences between its two possible enzyme transformations, what is the PubChem CID of the heaviest by molecular weight?"
- Gold: **4192** (Midazolam, MW 325.8).

**Annotator 경로 (약 2023년)**
1. PubChem Classification Browser → Food Additives and Ingredients → Food Additive Status에 filter를 걸면 HEXANE이 나옴.
2. hexane page의 10.6 Pharmacology and Biochemistry > Transformations에서 두 enzyme transformation(CYP2B6, CYP2E1)을 찾음.
3. 각 enzyme(gene)의 gene-chemical co-occurrence "full list"를 열고, 두 list에 공통인 chemical들의 MW를 비교함 → Midazolam.

**2026년 현재 데이터로 재구성한 결과** (fork 3개가 서로 다른 run의 messages.jsonl을 독립적으로 parse했고 결과가 일치함)
